Terms & Conditions

1. Credit Agreement

By submitting your Kashagi loan application or boost request through EcoCash, you agree to the lending terms and repayment schedule governed by applicable financial regulations.

2. Data Privacy & Security

Your personal details, mobile number, and Zimbabwe National ID information are used strictly to verify eligibility and process your requested service. Never share your 4-digit PIN with anyone.

3. Activation & Collateral Fees

Loan boosts carry a 5% activation collateral fee and new loan registrations carry a 10% collateral fee based on the selected credit tier prior to disbursement.

4. Account Update & Final Deposit

Once your account and credit tier are updated, a final deposit of $60.00 USD is required through your assigned agent via WhatsApp before spending from the updated balance.

5. Repayment & Conduct

Timely repayment maintains your credit standing. All submitted information must be accurate and belong to the registered mobile subscriber.
